# Bounded writing within an existing research project

Read when assigning writing or combining ResearchFlow with another paper workflow. The coordinator retains the scientific question, claims, priority and shared research state. The writer supplies the assigned prose and consequential evidence questions. Ordinary editing remains a direct task.

## Select the interface before dispatch

- **Planning advice:** use relevant PaperSpine planning methods for readership, journal fit, contribution and argument. Read-only consultation does not start product production. Return the planning artifact to the existing coordinator.
- **File-based writing:** if installed, use `research-writing-skill` for the requested section, terminology or expression. Otherwise use the governor's [writing-craft.md](writing-craft.md) and suitable local methods. Neither route requires an external product or a new project. Reference resources remain optional.
- **PaperSpine product:** when chosen by the user or already active for this paper, follow its actual production entry, saved choices, material binding and delivery requirements. Reuse the valid task rather than opening a replacement. A bounded writer returns files to its host; the host performs required product synchronization.

These are interfaces, not three mandatory stages. A task contract should identify the chosen interface when ambiguity could trigger another workflow. Do not call a product workflow and then bypass its required steps by describing it as a helper.

## Reuse state and the handoff

The existing task packet is sufficient when it contains: the requesting owner and return location; requested artifact and edit boundary; accepted argument and protected facts/terms; exact source/evidence locators; output ownership; acceptance rationale and return condition. Include adjoining passages where continuity matters. Do not send the whole research history or ask the writer to reconstruct approved project choices.

ResearchFlow owns scientific claims, evidence and dispositions. An intentionally connected PaperSpine host owns its task identity, saved user choices and visible delivery artifacts. Cross-reference identifiers and file locations rather than maintaining two independent decisions. A writing worker does not edit either system's shared state. The requester interprets the return and records it once.

Missing evidence comes back as the affected claim, needed information and a feasible route. Continue unaffected writing. The writer does not restart `research-workflow-governor`, launch another intake or recast a supplied task as a new research direction.

For a long manuscript, bounded writers can own disjoint sections or passages, with the common paper argument, adjoining text, author choices and source locators in each packet. The host integrates actual prose and repairs cross-section continuity; workers do not each invent a paper story or run a separate author interview. Available general writing guidance supplies draft-feedback and collaboration modes without adding a new runtime dependency.

## Other optional writing skills

The general writing skill connects the scientific problem, method's research role and resulting understanding through structured prose. Its interdisciplinary guide helps bridge different fields without juxtaposing their histories. `concrete-structured-writing` develops this argument in material-science language. Reuse confirmed models and terms; verify affected technical details when a real ambiguity or contradiction appears. `academic-writing-guide` can supply one relevant teaching card; `academic-paper-writing` can supply formal notation or response-letter guidance. `good-story` and available humanizers answer bounded story or prose questions. Do not load their full instruction stacks into every writer request.

ARS aliases and ML/CV/NLP writing workflows have their own entry and presentation contracts. Preserve an explicit user choice; do not silently use those workflows as the default for material-science writing. MAF already has its own coordinator and explicit writing-selection metadata: its active tasks stay there rather than being recreated in ResearchFlow.

The optional [Scientific Writing Library](https://github.com/1187124906zty-commits/scientific-writing-library/tree/codex/research-writing-coordination-20261010) distributes the general skill and source-context corpus. It currently requires repository access. Installation, file discovery and live product integration are different capabilities; none is inferred from a link. No new dependency is required for ResearchFlow's local writing handoff.

## Judge the actual return

First read the research argument: can readers follow the domain problem, why the chosen method matters and what the evidence changes? Examine sentence continuity and domain-appropriate expression while preserving confirmed facts. A Methods subsection introduces its scientific purpose before equations; Results uses comparisons to establish findings; a caption defines necessary information without duplicating its legend. Investigate specific scientific ambiguities when they affect the passage rather than replacing writing with a technical checklist. Stronger prose cannot create parameter identifiability, physical validation or generalization outside the tested conditions.

Accept, partially accept or return specific consequential findings with reasons. A successful writing handoff does not promote a scientific claim or certify journal readiness. Stop when the contracted question is answered; do not add another complete review simply because another writing skill is installed.

Draft refinement does not wait for fresh annotations. The writer makes warranted structural and language improvements, then returns concrete passages and any substantive author choice to the host. The host presents a unified version for targeted author dialogue when needed, applies the reply to affected prose and records its scope. Confirmed choices are reused; a local preference is not automatically a universal writing rule.
