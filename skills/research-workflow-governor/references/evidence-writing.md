# Scientific support at the writing handoff

Use when wording or figure organization changes a consequential scientific assertion. This file defines research judgments retained by the coordinator; writing practice belongs to `research-writing-skill` or the local [fallback](writing-craft.md).

Read the original evidence that bears on the assertion. Preserve the scientific object, reference or baseline, conditions, data role and relevant contrary observations. A literature excerpt reports its source study; an expression example supplies no evidence for the current study. Distinguish an observed result, mathematical consequence, model-consistent explanation, diagnosed cause and extrapolation.

| Evidence available | Assertion supported | Additional support for a broader assertion |
|---|---|---|
| Approximation theorem under stated assumptions | Representational capability in its declared norm and conditions | Finite-data learning and optimizer performance need separate analysis |
| Matched observations and predictions | Agreement for the measured output over the tested comparison | Other fields or mechanisms require their own observations and discriminating tests |
| Ranking on declared benchmarks | Performance under the tested metrics and computation conditions | Broader superiority requires corresponding comparisons and tradeoffs |
| Different parameter sets fitting comparably | Ambiguity for those observations | Unique identification requires informative observations and appropriate analysis |
| Prediction on a declared test split | Performance on that split and response range | New material relations or conditions require distinct tests |
| Fast prediction after training | Speed for the stated prediction workload | Overall efficiency also depends on data generation, training and amortization |
| Small equation residual | Satisfaction of the checked relation to the measured tolerance | Other observables and real-world validity remain separate questions |

Judge the complete assertion and its conditions, rather than assigning verbs a universal strength ranking. A tested improvement may be established directly while its cause remains uncertain. Fluent prose, source prestige and completed query fields cannot increase the attained evidence level.

When evidence is missing, the writer returns the affected assertion, needed comparison or observable, available originals and a feasible bounded investigation. The coordinator decides the research action, records its disposition through the existing task interface and keeps unaffected writing active. Editorial acceptance closes a writing task; claim promotion requires an evidence-specific scientific judgment.

Source distinctions were checked against the Scientific Writing Library’s located entries G103 (`10.1038/nphys3424`), EXP-NMI-P010/P012 (`10.1038/s42256-026-01233-9`), EXP-NMI-P006/P008 (`10.1038/s42256-023-00762-x`) and EXP-NPCM-P007 (`10.1038/s41524-026-02054-5`). Use the library’s declared source versions to recover exact locations. These examples explain distinctions; they are not default evidence for a new manuscript.
