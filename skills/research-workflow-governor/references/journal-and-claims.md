# Journal readers and evidence-shaped writing

Use original journal instructions and related published papers as evidence. Verify title, year, DOI/official URL and passages actually used. Publisher full text through an existing lawful session can use `sciencedirect-live-session-fetcher` when installed and applicable. Search a handful of representative papers with different methods or outcomes rather than choosing only a single admired template.

Record a compact journal profile: scope and reader questions; expected type of contribution; typical evidence/comparisons; section and figure logic; terminology/notation conventions; explicit submission requirements; sources and sample limits. Distinguish editorial requirements from inferred conventions and personal preferences. Reassess fit after results change.

The argument outline should connect each main claim with the evidence, assumptions, figure/table and limitation that permit it. A result gap is a question for a bounded evidence task, not a blank to be filled with plausible prose. Keep observed/calibrated/predicted/validated language distinct. Interpret contradictory results rather than removing them.

Use [writing-craft.md](writing-craft.md) for readable paragraph handoffs and contextual source learning. Library examples teach argument functions and language relationships; their source anchors do not support the current study's findings. Select by the reader decision and inspect original context when it matters. The detailed [evidence-writing.md](evidence-writing.md) is optional for evidence/library operations. Corpus acquisition targets, sample sizes and source-specific models do not become general research requirements.

When writing exposes a consequential data gap, preserve the intended question and actively initiate supplementary research within the user's authorization before defaulting to reduced scope. The writer supplies the affected claim, missing comparison/observable, original inputs, fixed conditions and desired decision information; the coordinator registers a bounded domain task and tracks its return. Where the host and user support separate conversations, use the actual task/project adapter and origin thread ID, including human-authorized return messaging. Simulation specialists execute and review new evidence in owned paths; the writing task waits on dependent findings while continuing unrelated work. Inspect and record the actual return before revising methods, displays, interpretation and conclusions together. Unknown-completion external work is reconciled before replay. Pending data does not authorize invented findings or final completion; a negative result changes the argument after evidence has been assessed.

The final evidence callback follows completed domain acceptance: actual execution and applicable integrity checks, observable recovery, effect-specific adequacy, independent evidence review and the domain coordinator's disposition. It distinguishes supported, unresolved and rejected findings. A completed negative investigation can be accepted as evidence without accepting its hypothesis. Intermediate progress stays in the execution conversation unless requested; it cannot be used to close the manuscript's evidence dependency. Genuine access failures and required human decisions remain reportable before final acceptance.

Before a major multi-agent revision, agree the concrete predecessor → unresolved object/aspect → present design → evidence → answer chain. If a scientific noun or comparison is ambiguous, identify its actual source, measurement population/model, observable and baseline before editing its name. A new term or acronym needs a distinct useful definition; it does not establish novelty.

Assign chapter responsibilities by dependencies. A Methods owner and an integrated Results/Discussion owner can preserve continuity through reciprocal checks of quantities, controls, data roles and observation definitions. Keep local interpretation beside its evidence when a separate discussion would repeat it; retain a distinct Discussion when cross-result synthesis has a substantive purpose. Introduction derives motivation from the accepted lineage and design, Discussion/Conclusions close the same question, and final title/abstract use the whole argument. Adapt chapter names and Introduction priorities to experimental, numerical, theoretical or mixed evidence rather than cataloguing tools. The coordinator checks outgoing/incoming section points and closes actual repairs.

For a major revision, identify the primary research paradigm and the argument before assigning prose. Give writers the controlling points and needed original sources, then inspect their actual paragraph progression and design decisions at return. The Introduction should narrow the chosen research line; the abstract should connect the needed capability, actual approach and grouped findings; Methods should make model adoption, formulation premises and comparison identities recoverable. Distinguish section, subsection and paragraph openings or closures by the information the reader needs at that position. Define scientific schemes and parameter roles before referring to their codes, and move repetitive inventories to a useful table or retrievable supplement. These are comprehension checks, not fixed paragraph counts, sentence recipes, table requirements or new study-specific scientific rules. Use the relevant short section guidance actually delivered by the MAF loader; a link or a completed style checklist alone does not demonstrate its application.

Before drafting, chapter owners study the current author guide and relevant passages in comparable articles. Return located requirements, observed conventions and the justified adaptation separately. Give authorized human reviewers the guide, manuscript and source index/full texts available for their reading; record supplied, awaiting review and actual feedback distinctly. Supply each agent its relevant sources rather than the entire historical library. Reference coverage follows the argument, article type and current user target; sample counts and article-type-specific limits are not universal journal rules.

For whole-paper review, inspect the full section/subsection list and paragraph controlling points as a reverse outline. Judge whether it reveals the research route, not just an execution list. Section openings should make otherwise missing motivation or dependencies clear; they are conditional scientific bridges, not mandatory roadmaps. Place consequential adequacy evidence before the inference that needs it, while Methods explains the assessment procedure. At weak transitions, identify subjects/actions, unique antecedents and how new information connects to known objects; also check whether the paragraph stays on its controlling point. Express necessary contrast, consequence or qualification with an accurate connector; removing formulaic wording must not remove the relation. Detailed methods and located source guidance are maintained in the independent [MAF writing skills](https://github.com/1187124906zty-commits/research-assistant-maf/blob/codex/maf-reconstruction/docs/writing-skills.zh.md).

For a weak effect, decide whether the claim is descriptive, uncertain or unsupported based on uncertainty and checks; do not automatically demand heroic accuracy. For a strong claimed effect with a failed implementation or condition mismatch, more persuasive writing cannot establish it. A negative finding can become the contribution if the comparison and boundary are credible.

Review the draft from an informed adjacent reader's perspective: What question is answered? Why this method? What is compared? What supports the explanation? Where does it hold? How could I reproduce or challenge it? Also check sources and figure/data revisions, calibration/validation roles, and whether abstract/conclusion exceed the tested scope. Report submission readiness separately from scientific truth and from layout completion.
