# A question-to-manuscript workflow

These are dependencies and feedback opportunities, not a waterfall that forbids exploration. Keep a small living manuscript outline from early work, with unknown results clearly marked as unknown in internal notes. Do not turn internal notes into a publishable result.

1. **Investigate the actual problem.** Recover the user's motivation and constraints. Inspect available data, software and prior work. Distinguish a scientific uncertainty from a tool or collaboration defect. Set a tentative problem and a question that a feasible first observation can answer.
2. **Establish the research line and audience.** Search real primary papers and relevant reviews; inspect pivotal methods in full. Reconstruct how the question, assumptions, measurements and explanations evolved. PaperSpine profiles candidate journals from current aims/scope, author instructions and a small purposeful sample of related recent papers. A sample suggests conventions; it does not define an immutable style rule.
3. **Frame a provisional contribution.** State what a reader will learn, why the comparison or explanation matters, and what evidence would disprove it. A useful application or negative boundary may be enough; do not impose algorithm novelty. Make a claim-to-evidence-to-figure outline and compare at least plausible alternative explanations. Select a primary journal and fallback with reasons and unresolved fit questions.
4. **Choose tools through a pilot.** Ask what each tool can represent, its limiting assumptions, needed input, outputs and costs. Use a benchmark, one control case, small experiment or data screen before a large campaign. Preserve units, conditions and provenance. A pilot may change the research question or show the proposed method cannot answer it.
5. **Produce evidence in bounded increments.** Verification, condition matching, calibration, independent validation, parameter analysis and uncertainty address distinct questions. Acquire or rerun only what supports a planned claim or distinguishes mechanisms. Reassess at useful returns; do not endlessly refine. Negative results return to contribution and method choice.
6. **Interpret and revise the argument.** Compare predictions, quantify effects relative to relevant errors, look for alternatives and counterexamples. Ask for a targeted new diagnostic only if it resolves meaningful ambiguity. Separate observed association, model-consistent mechanism, causal identification and generalization. Update the living outline, claims and scope.
7. **Write for the chosen readers.** PaperSpine joins methods, evidence, mechanism and contribution in an intelligible paper. Use [writing-craft.md](writing-craft.md) to connect the reader's question, contextual examples, evidence and displays. Draft complete sections from actual results and checked sources; retain conditions that change the inference and put reproduction inventories where readers can retrieve them. When writing exposes a consequential gap, return its affected question and informative comparison to step 5 while continuing unaffected prose. The optional [evidence-writing.md](evidence-writing.md) retains detailed evidence/library operations when required.
8. **Independent review and targeted repair.** Review question, comparison, reproducibility, evidence strength, mechanisms, scope, journal fit and clarity. Categorize issues by affected claim and severity. Fix claim-invalidating defects first, then major explanation gaps and required format; stop discretionary polish once it adds little. Inspect compiled/rendered outputs when format requires it.

At each return, update the evolving question, known facts, rejected hypotheses, uncertainty and next decision. Accepted evidence can lead back to any earlier step. Preserve why the change was made. A paper-ready package includes manuscript, figures/tables, checked references, methods/code/data availability, evidence map and unresolved limitations. Uploading the tool or drafting the manuscript is not submitting the paper.
