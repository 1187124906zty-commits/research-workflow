# Constructive research progression

Use when evidence has not answered the intended question or established a worthwhile contribution. Organize active investigation; do not require favorable results.

Name the sought contribution: a useful performance improvement, a discriminated explanation, a new regime or relationship, or a defensible evaluation/decision method. Distinguish it from what current data establish. A failed accuracy claim does not become a mechanism discovery without discriminating evidence.

First inspect usable existing data. Form plausible competing explanations and distinguishing predictions. Choose a feasible comparison that removes an important ambiguity at reasonable cost. More samples, components or grid refinement are useful when their role in that decision is clear. New ideas may combine established methods; assess mechanism and validation rather than requiring an identical precedent.

Before a consequential run, state quantities, observation rules, comparison conditions, calibration/tuning budget and evaluation data. Accuracy, efficiency and robustness need different comparisons. Numerical settings should resolve the relevant effect, rather than satisfy a universal tolerance inherited from a prior case.

Return gains, tradeoffs, uncertainty, counterexamples, cost and changed understanding. Operational failures call for repair; unfavorable scientific results call for hypothesis revision or informative tests. Two attempts without changed understanding trigger coordinator reassessment, not automatic acceptance or abandonment.

Discovery may adapt hypotheses and models. Confirmation fixes the candidate and tests evidence not used for selection. Viewed data keep their original role. Preserve all attempts and selection history. When independent data are inaccessible, report the narrower comparison and investigate useful remaining questions instead of relabeling the same data independent.

Maintain a small question–comparison–finding–display map. For each planned display, identify the judgment, control, inputs and required support. Predefine metrics and selection rules for consequential comparisons; layout may develop as understanding changes. Clear presentation exposes the actual gain, failure or distinction rather than manufacturing visual contrast.

Close with a located decision: adopt an improvement within demonstrated scope, retain a conditional explanation, reject a route, or identify what remains unidentifiable. An informative finding can complete a bounded task while a larger performance goal stays open. Do not complete substantive research merely because its limitations have been polished into fluent prose.
