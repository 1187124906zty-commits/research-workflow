# From a research decision to a readable paper

Use for consequential writing handoffs. The coordinator supplies the question, evidence and intended contribution; the writer develops the argument and prose. Project-specific physics and numerical conditions stay in the project packet.

## Give the writer a question, not an inventory

Identify what readers should understand after the passage, how it follows the preceding point, and what the next passage adds. Supply original evidence and comparison identities. Distinguish the study objective, performed operation and earned finding. Editorial acceptance does not establish scientific support.

An abstract introduces the recognizable problem, answering comparison and main result; retain a procedural detail only when it changes that inference. The Introduction explains why the question matters, which prior findings leave it unresolved and how the study responds. Methods introduces an adopted or formulated model through its purpose before using definite short references. Results develops findings through comparisons; Discussion explains implications, alternatives and consequential limits. These are functions, not fixed paragraph counts or phrase quotas.

## Learn from contextual exemplars

The [Scientific Writing Library](https://github.com/1187124906zty-commits/scientific-writing-library) provides expression records. Its `general/contextual-exemplars.json` and `examples/contextual-exemplars.md` add paragraph and figure cards. Select a few by the reasoning relationship and section; inspect original context when adaptation depends on it. Adapt the progression, not the source's scientific assertion. Source prestige and expression frequency do not establish universal suitability.

A useful example explains context, sentence subjects, verbs, referents, connectors and why each sentence adds information. Label authored teaching paragraphs hypothetical; locate source quotations. Query metadata supports retrieval, but completed slots cannot certify truth, comprehension or naturalness. Keep schemas and host APIs in tool adapters.

This **hypothetical teaching paragraph** establishes a tension before a route:

> Direct simulations resolve the effect of microstructure on transport, but their cost limits repeated parameter studies. Reduced models make these studies more affordable, provided that the retained representation preserves the response of interest. We develop a reduced formulation around that response and assess its accuracy, computational cost and sensitivity to the training conditions.

It advances from capability to cost, response and evaluation. It is not evidence that the current model succeeds. Prefer a concrete subject and action over chains such as “the implementation of the consideration of”. Choose connectors for the actual comparison, concession, consequence, explanation or sequence.

## Return to research when contribution is missing

Return a consequential gap with its affected question, available data, alternatives and informative comparison. Within authorized research scope, the coordinator investigates feasible improvements or discriminating discoveries before accepting a manuscript that merely inventories deficiencies. ## Design displays as evidence

For a figure or table, state the judgment it should enable and the control that makes that judgment meaningful. Assign panels to a progression or distinct comparisons. Keep reference/candidate/condition roles consistent across displays, supplemented by symbols, line styles or direct labels. Match scales for magnitude comparisons; disclose necessary changes.

Uncertainty bands, seed distributions, measurement intervals and selected best cases answer different questions. Name their estimators, populations and selection rules. A field map exposes structure; reference/error views assess agreement; an ablation or diagnostic may support an explanation. They do not automatically replace one another.

Comparison tables expose conditions, data roles, degrees of freedom, gains and costs; parameter tables support reproduction. Keep decisive evidence near its interpretation, moving exact inventories to supplements when useful. No fixed figure count or text-to-figure ratio follows.

## Close distinct reviews

Language review reconstructs reader understanding and information flow. Scientific review checks comparisons, evidence and alternatives. Visual review checks data, displays, captions and text together. Share consequential findings; do not give every role the same repeated instruction stack.

Inspect compiled pages and adjacent passages. Sentences should advance a controlling point while retaining necessary qualifications. Record consequential questions and requester dispositions in ordinary outputs, not a form per sentence. Software tests and runtime audits check execution and binding, not publication quality.
