# Writing when no writing skill is available

Use this short fallback only when the assigned writer cannot use `research-writing-skill`. The coordinator still owns scientific decisions; [writing-integration.md](writing-integration.md) defines the handoff.

Identify the paper’s central scientific task and what the assigned passage must add. Draft continuous prose from actual evidence. The Introduction develops the unresolved difficulty through prior capabilities and conditions; Methods explains the adopted components and their connection before the necessary definitions; Results establishes findings through comparisons; Discussion interprets their value and consequential limits. These functions do not prescribe paragraph counts or a universal structure.

Keep scientific objects and terms stable. Make sentence subjects, actions and referents clear, and connect new information to the preceding premise. State a supported judgment directly, retaining the condition that makes it true. Condense repeated explanations while preserving the background needed to understand the method or finding. Select exemplars by their reasoning relation and context rather than vocabulary overlap.

Read the actual figures with the prose. Use each display to establish a comparison or relation; a caption defines information needed to read the display without repeating its legend or every method step. Preserve complete evidence in retrievable artifacts, while the main text explains decisive observations and what they mean.

Read the revised passage in its neighboring context and repair actual discontinuities. Return the text, relevant source locations and consequential evidence questions. For a long manuscript, the coordinator integrates disjoint outputs and records coverage; a locally fluent section does not establish a coherent paper.
