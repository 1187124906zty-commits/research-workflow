## Passages and citation integrity

Hou p8 states that the reduced-order model is calibrated with CFD to compute time-temperature trajectories of fluid elements. Its p9 describes tracer particles and their time-temperature profiles; p10 contrasts CFD with conduction (near-surface peak narrowed by surface-driven motion; subsurface increased by convection/recoil depression). §4.7 p13 explicitly calculates Eagar–Tsai temperature first, then scales it for surface-tension-driven convection with Appendix C. Paragraph A reflects these statements without importing its measured/model-specific dimensions, speeds, oxide kinetics or quantitative thermal accuracy.

Hosaeus abstract states resistive volume heating in a capacitor discharge circuit, submicrosecond time resolution, mutual enthalpy/resistivity/temperature/density relations in solid and liquid, and conductivity estimated via Wiedemann–Franz at the end of solid/beginning of liquid. Paragraph B uses only this bounded route. No claim of full-text reading is made.

Plotkowski abstract states semi-analytical transient heat conduction, theoretical verification for AlSi10Mg SLM and IN718 EBM simple geometries, comparison with Rosenthal, and experimental grain-structure comparison for two IN718 point-melt strategies. E5 uses the achievement only. Detailed Methods/Results remain unread by this reviewer.
