# R2: from scientific tension to a complete research draft

This revision applies a bounded multi-agent research-to-paper method to existing AMMT IN625 evidence. The paper's central tension is the practical use of geometric calibration versus the constraints needed for credible thermal interpretation. Existing achievements and prior geometry/temperature ambiguity are credited before the contribution is narrowed to a controlled high-temperature closure diagnosis.

The coordinated route actually produced a 22-page English first draft with 39 cited sources and five scientific figures. It reused six unique production solutions; there were no new PDE solves. Geometry residuals, closure effects and rear spatial/material-time interpretation now form one argument rather than unrelated analyses.

Native MAF executed two real writer calls. The Introduction returned; the Discussion wrote candidates but exceeded 1500 seconds before final JSON, so native review/requester nodes did not execute in that batch. Codex specialists and the coordinator completed the subsequent section exchange, integration and whole-paper review. These are separate provenance facts. This case demonstrates useful coordinated production and an actual runtime limitation; it is not evidence of a fully autonomous research pipeline, lower cost or improved publication probability.

The final scientific review recommends a complete, evidence-bounded draft for human review. Physical thermal validation, sufficient contribution for the target journal, responsible human author metadata and current journal-specific submission rules remain unestablished. Third-party full texts, publisher-page renders, private manifests and runtime histories are excluded from the public package.
