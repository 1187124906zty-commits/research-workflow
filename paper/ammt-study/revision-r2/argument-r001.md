# Editorial argument for the IN625 single-track study

Coordinator scientific/editing decision after reading the R2 research line and evidence candidates; journal profile is bound separately before dispatch. Target: an English research first draft for Additive Manufacturing readers. Existing PDE solutions only. No major-method novelty is promised.

The scientific tension is the practical dependence on affordable, geometrically calibrated thermal models versus the need for trustworthy solidification-relevant interpretation. Melt-pool geometry is observable and useful, but source calibration, material closure and observation definitions jointly influence what agreement means. Published research already demonstrates parameter compensation, geometry/temperature ambiguity and regime-dependent conduction failure. The opening must establish this shared problem with actual literature, rather than announce an unprecedented gap or lead with a tiny variable question.

The useful increment here is a controlled diagnosis in one nonlinear enthalpy case: keep an existing length calibration fixed, separate the two high-temperature property continuations, and distinguish shape discrepancy, common rear-boundary displacement, phase-span separation and speed-derived material time. These are related outputs with different inferential roles. The study does not prove that equally fitted branches have different thermal histories because these branches are not recalibrated to equal geometry.

Ross, Kusano/Watanabe and Coleman provide successful calibrated-source interpolation/transfer under their own conditions. Whalen and Coleman already use temperature-dependent properties and phase treatment. Credit these achievements; do not construct a strawman that simplified models universally fail or all use constant properties. Wells's unread solidification-path study limits any priority claim. Describe the positive case contribution without first/only language.

Reference coverage follows scientific functions and comparable article scope. Pursue at least 38 genuinely used, verified relevant sources as the provisional project target; inspect any shortfall by missing argument coverage. Broad operando/multiphase samples with 69-72 references are format/reader exemplars, not a mechanical minimum for this six-solution diagnostic case. No count is an editorial or scientific PASS.

## Section promises and their evidence

- Introduction: establish why thermal interpretation matters, what conduction/calibration/measurement research already achieves, and why an aggregate geometry or joint-property response leaves interpretation incomplete. Narrow to a modest decomposition of closure response; explicitly credit recent 2026 near neighbours.
- Methods: the one-target/fixed-parameter design comes first; model assumptions, equations, properties, boundary conditions, numerical algorithm and distinct observation operators follow. Define the four-corner design and rear thresholds before interpreting them. Retain quantitative verification and reproducibility in the appropriate main/appendix location.
- Results: first show what the single length agreement fails to transfer to width/depth; next decompose high-temperature conductivity/storage effects and tail translation; finally link the rear spatial interval to speed-dependent material time. Preserve four original evidence figures and add the actual property-continuation figure if legible.
- Discussion: compare these specific findings with near neighbours; explain latent-inclusive storage and conductivity response without asserting exclusive bulk causality; discuss source/observation/missing physics alternatives and a useful next discriminating comparison. Answer the Introduction rather than restating the numbers.
- Conclusions: close the original tension with the earned result: fitted geometry alone does not establish closure-robust thermal interpretation, and this controlled decomposition makes the different sources of response explicit. Summarize what changed in understanding, with a few decisive quantitative anchors.
- Title/abstract: communicate thermal credibility after geometric calibration, not a list of technical settings. Abstract uses background/tension, the bounded design, the main response and meaning. Select essential numbers, keep detailed tolerances/branches/uncertainty arithmetic in the body.

## Evidence boundaries that change interpretation

1. Actual FiPy conservative moving-frame enthalpy model; coordinate transport is not liquid flow. No evaporation/free-surface/melt momentum. Bare IN625 plate, three AMMT conditions, six shared unique production solutions.
2. B length is fitted at eta=0.2890548519203547. B width/depth are excluded diagnostics; A/C fixed-parameter comparisons are retrospective and nonblind. Do not imply prospective validation.
3. Imaging length and maximum-temperature fusion width/depth have different observation operators and populations. Decimal NIST width/depth and separately sourced Lane expanded uncertainties cannot form a joint likelihood or coverage claim.
4. Conductivity input ends at 982 C and sensible cp at 1093 C, both below Ts=1290 C. L/H are deterministic extrapolation choices, not liquid properties or probability bounds.
5. Fixed-B k holding changes length by +43.21 micrometres, cp holding by -6.17 and interaction by -2.76; joint +34.28. Rear solidus/liquidus shift together; k-only rear solidus accounts for 98.3% of length change. All corners remain too wide/shallow at B.
6. At 1320 C k changes by -22.48%; sensible cp by -7.09%, latent-inclusive effective cp by -0.95%. This matched-temperature comparison supports a bounded model interpretation, not a complete causal proof. k also affects surface recovery. State-selected Pe/flux populations differ; HL rear outward diffusive flux rises from 14.724 to 14.913 W, so lower k does not establish lower integrated regional heat flow.
7. Baseline B/C axial changes in L are 0.361/0.199 micrometres. They support the major tail/shape scale comparison but are not complete error bounds, especially for small factorial interactions. Held-k peak temperatures are sensitivity outputs outside credible physical-temperature prediction.
8. B/C spans 80.26/80.79 micrometres map to 100.33/67.33 microseconds. Derived interval cooling rate changes 49.02%; this is the coordinate mapping, not a measured extra validation endpoint or microstructure prediction.
