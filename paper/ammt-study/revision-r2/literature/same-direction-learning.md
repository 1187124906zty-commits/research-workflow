## Supplementary comparisons that revise the argument

[Whalen2021](pdfs/whalen2021.pdf) and [Coleman2024](pdfs/dynamic2024.pdf) show that “previous models use constant properties” is too broad. Whalen explicitly incorporates temperature-dependent material/powder properties and uncertainty; Coleman uses different solid/liquid property functions, latent storage and a eutectic freezing endpoint. Coleman and [Kusano/Watanabe2024](pdfs/heatsourcebayes2024.pdf) also provide successful calibrated-source transfer under their conditions. The paper should therefore introduce its continuation experiment as a deliberate controlled restriction within one model, rather than a missing capability of all simplified thermal models.

Myers2023 remains the direct precedent for geometry-fitting parameter combinations with different temperature profiles. The current study does not perform equal-fit recalibration of each closure branch. Its useful additional distinctions are common rear-boundary translation versus phase-span change, and spatial span versus speed-derived passage time. Neither distinction should be claimed as globally unprecedented while the Wells solidification-path original remains unread.
