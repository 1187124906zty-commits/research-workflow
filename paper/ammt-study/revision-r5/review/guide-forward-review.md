## Located sample findings

Severity labels denote the affected meaning, not a universal journal rejection standard. All proposed wording is a **minimal repair objective**, not an instruction to rewrite the entire section.

### S1 — Calibration target: one track versus a condition mean

- **Location:** title-abstract.tex L3, “retaining the source-power fraction fitted to one track length.”
- **Evidence:** R5 sources/report.md / Comparator definitions identifies Lane Table4 B=359 μm as the thermographic **condition mean**; length observations are video-frame populations, with B N=10. verified-data `/calibration` and `/experimental_roles` retain B length as the calibration target. The candidate Discussion L6 correctly says “B's thermographic mean length.”
- **Issue/claim affected:** “one track length” can suggest a single physical track or specimen observation; the actual target is one condition's aggregate length. This obscures the fit's data role and is inconsistent with the clearer candidate Discussion.
- **Severity/status:** medium reader/data-role ambiguity; no numerical error established.
- **Smallest repair:** say “fitted to one condition's mean thermographic length” or “fitted to the B thermographic mean length.” Abstract need not list all population counts.
- **Recheck:** integrated Methods and abstract identify the same B mean and retain retrospective nonblind comparisons; do not turn it into a newly reserved validation track.

### S2 — Specific-heat percentage and integrated enthalpy are different quantities

- **Location:** discussion-conclusions.tex L15, “the latter change is only 0.95% of latent-inclusive effective specific heat and 0.66% of enthalpy from the cold reference.”
- **Evidence:** at 1320 °C, original/report matched-property records and factorial-summary `/matched_temperature_properties` give LL/LH sensible cp=721.126126/670 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹, effective cp=5387.792793/5336.666667 J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹ and H=7398326139.039039/7349350377.777778 J m⁻³. Read-only recomputation gives 7.089762%, 0.948925% and 0.661984% decreases, respectively. property-math-check independently defines the integrated enthalpy difference as 5802.815315 J kg⁻¹, not the pointwise cp decrement.
- **Issue/claim affected:** the sensible-cp change can be normalized by effective cp, but the phrase also normalizes that same “latter change” by enthalpy. Their units differ. The 0.66% number is a correct fractional **integrated enthalpy reduction caused by the changed function**, not the pointwise cp decrement as a fraction of H.
- **Severity/status:** medium quantitative semantic/dimensional defect, even though all three numerical percentages are correct.
- **Smallest repair:** split the relation: “The change reduces latent-inclusive effective specific heat by 0.95% at 1320 °C; integrating the altered sensible-heat function gives a 0.66% reduction in enthalpy from the cold reference.” Keep the beginning-below-solidus fact.
- **Recheck:** nouns explicitly name cp_eff versus H and each percentage has its own denominator; no new latent-heat intervention is introduced.

### S3 — A fixed source fraction is not fixed source power across A/B/C

- **Location:** discussion-conclusions.tex L40 final sentence, “their model-based comparison under a fixed source and phase law.”
- **Evidence:** research_case_metrics A/B/C powers are 137.9/179.2/179.2 W and speeds 0.4/0.8/1.2 m/s; original factorial report scope is fixed-B retained η. The source is q ∝ ηP, with η=0.2890548519 retained; the field/absorbed input changes with P across A/B/C. Both application candidate L6 and frozen Methods distinguish fitted fraction from source power.
- **Issue/claim affected:** this closing sentence applies to the set of geometric, boundary and B/C time results. “fixed source” can imply fixed power or an unchanged source field for all conditions; only factor/shape convention/phase law are common across operating conditions. The four B cases do retain identical source power.
- **Severity/status:** medium scope/comparator ambiguity; the Results design itself is not found incorrect.
- **Smallest repair:** “with a retained calibrated source-power fraction and a fixed phase law,” or explicitly restrict fixed source power to the four-case B contrast.
- **Recheck:** final summary does not imply that A/B/C differ solely in scan speed, and fixed-B material contrast remains separate from operating-condition response.

### S4 — Identify Lane's interval/operator rather than an ambiguous plural antecedent

- **Location:** discussion-conclusions.tex L22, after Hooper and Lane: “Their temperature range and radiance operator differ from the present liquidus-to-solidus calculation.”
- **Evidence:** Lane original P32–P34 specifically defines 1290→1190 °C (and optional 1290→1000 °C) cooling estimates and a radiance/emittance mapping. - **Issue/claim affected:** “Their” may refer to Lane et al. alone, or both Hooper and Lane; the second reading generalizes the same range/operator to both source methods. The following comparison needs Lane's actual definition.
- **Severity/status:** low-to-medium attribution/reader ambiguity; not a finding that either cited experiment is incorrectly described in its own preceding sentence.
- **Smallest repair:** name “Lane's cooling interval and radiance-based observation mapping…” at this point.
- **Recheck:** the explicit comparison is Lane versus the modeled 1350→1290 °C interval; Hooper remains evidence for transient observation histories, not a shared radiance measurand.

### S5 — Keep the proposed diagnostics as two distinguishable operations

- **Location:** discussion-conclusions.tex L17, “Separating a fixed-region energy budget from this recovery contribution would be needed…”
- **Evidence:** original factorial report and frozen Methods/Appendix change k, its primitive K and surface recovery together; rear-region integration selects T≥Ts and ξ<0 separately in each solved branch. - **Issue/claim affected:** current wording can sound like one operation separates an energy budget from a recovery displacement, rather than two complementary tests. It makes the proposed distinguishing evidence harder to execute.
- **Severity/status:** low-to-medium reader/implementation gap in proposed future diagnostic; current results still correctly avoid unique causal attribution.
- **Smallest repair:** “A matched-region flux comparison, together with a separate decomposition of field-response and surface-recovery contributions, would be needed…” Do not claim either test has already been run or fixes the experimental shape.
- **Recheck:** each proposed operation is named with its own question; a narrower alternative wording is acceptable if it communicates the same distinction.
