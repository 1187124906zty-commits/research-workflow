### R5-F01 — Retained source fraction is called retained source power across A/B/C

**Severity:** minor factual condition error; required text repair. **Affected claim:** the operating-condition comparison design. The four-case B constitutive contrast legitimately retains source power, whereas the A/B/C comparison retains the fitted fraction.

**Located evidence:** frozen L57, PDF p. 3, says: “The first retains the B-fitted source power when assessing width, depth and the other operating conditions.” Frozen L154, PDF pp. 6–7, ends: “This length fit sets the source power for the subsequent geometry and property comparisons.” Table 1 records A/B/C applied powers of 137.9/179.2/179.2 W. The power therefore differs for A, although eta is retained. The fixed-power B/C speed contrast and all four B property branches are correctly defined elsewhere.

**Consequence:** a reader following the Methods roadmap could interpret A/B/C as a pure speed comparison at one source power, contrary to the actual inputs. The detailed design and Results correctly distinguish A, so this is repairable wording rather than evidence of the wrong numerical experiment.

**Smallest useful repair:** in L57, say that the first comparison retains the B-fitted **effective source-power fraction** across operating conditions. In the final sentence of L154, distinguish retaining eta across A/B/C from fixing source power in the four B property cases. No solver run, new experiment, refit, new section, or numerical precision change is needed. The subsection heading at L153 and fixed-power descriptions explicitly restricted to B can remain.
