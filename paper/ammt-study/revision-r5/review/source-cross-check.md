## Smallest necessary repairs

### R1 — narrow the present scalar-adjustment conclusion

**Location:** Discussion L300, sentence beginning “Their results establish the limitations of a scalar source adjustment within each model”. **Priority:** required scientific scope repair; localized wording only.

The predecessor explicitly investigates its remaining scalar freedom, whereas the present production design fits η to B length and retains it. The present residual establishes failure of that length-fitted result to reproduce transverse means; it does not establish that every alternative scalar η is incapable of a joint fit. The adjacent text correctly retains the opposite residual signs and model differences.

Replace only that sentence with:

> The length-fitted scalar adjustments leave transverse discrepancies in both models, while the opposite residual directions prevent assigning the present discrepancy to a mechanism inferred from the predecessor.

This preserves the comparison and its implication without adding a scalar-search result that was not obtained.

### R2 — specify the discrimination supplied by future diagnostics

**Location:** Discussion L309, the two future sentences beginning “A conductive-power comparison…” and “Separately evaluating…”. **Priority:** recommended precision improvement, not a finding that the manuscript reports unexecuted work as completed.

The paragraph already rejects a simple reduced-heat-escape explanation, correctly reports the rising selected-region flux and uses future tense. The proposed matched-region integral controls boundary selection but does not hold gradients or the temperature field fixed. Likewise, a separately controlled recovery calculation can assess a numerical observation contribution without identifying a unique physical mechanism.

An exact small replacement is:

> A conductive-power comparison over an identical spatial region would remove the changing integration boundary from the heat-escape comparison. Separately re-evaluating surface-temperature recovery would assess its contribution to boundary displacement. These diagnostics have not been performed.

No new numerical claim or new solve is necessary. If the coordinator prefers to retain the current future-tense wording, the execution-status part of the contracted check still passes; the more definite “would identify” is the avoidable overpromise.
