# Actual MAF inputs and requester integration

This manuscript used a native Microsoft Agent Framework graph with the project's real `CodexProvider`, rather than a deterministic demo provider. Two workers started from frozen copies of `evidence-dossier.md` and `verified-data.json`: a mechanism analyst and a Results/Discussion writer. Each had separate owned outputs, a finite attempt budget and fresh model context. The workflow then obtained separate evidence reviews and coordinator dispositions. Full article assembly, journal literature, rendering and final actual-manuscript review were performed in the parent task.

The numerical case predates this session and was generated with SimAgent. No new PDE calculations were performed by the manuscript tasks.

## Contributions used in the manuscript

- The writer's numerical synthesis supported the Results progression: calibration-conditioned shape discrepancy → passage-time distinction → constitutive competition → limits. Root supplied the verified citations, complete Methods and final prose.
- The writer review found an overbroad speed attribution spanning A/B/C. The bounded second attempt restricted it to fixed-power B/C. The final article explicitly distinguishes A/B, where power changes, from B/C.
- The mechanism review identified a consequential inference limit: changing conductivity changes both bulk diffusion and the conductivity-dependent surface-temperature inverse. Root added this distinction to Discussion and did not claim an isolated bulk-field cause.
- The local Péclet populations are temperature-selected in different solutions; reduced k does not prove a reduction in integrated regional heat flux. Root added the discrete face statistic and avoided physical liquid-flow attribution.
- Numerical checks remain claim-specific: major length responses versus baseline axial sensitivity; small width/depth interactions as current-mesh arithmetic. No manuscript artifact was promoted to physical-validation evidence.

## Actual interruption and reconciliation

The second writer finished its localized sentence repair but returned Markdown-style line and JSON-fragment pointers in `evidence.path`, plus a session-only review path. The runtime expects actual declared file paths, so it rejected binding and stopped. Root inspected the already completed SDK thread and repaired output, preserved the raw JSON, normalized the locators to their declared files, and excluded the non-file session review from the evidence array. The documented `reconcile` path imported that inspected return. It did not rerun the writer or weaken scientific checks. The workflow then performed its own focused review and requester disposition.

The publicly saved [MAF case](https://github.com/1187124906zty-commits/research-assistant-maf/tree/codex/maf-reconstruction/examples/ammt-manuscript) preserves the real outputs, review returns, decisions and recovery record. It demonstrates this bounded software path, including explicit intervention. It is not a controlled benchmark of research productivity or proof that all future sessions complete autonomously.

The first mechanism reviewer deliberately inspected evidence before producer interpretation, and disclosed that it had not checked the producer artifact. The writing reviewers read their actual section. Root read both outputs. A separate article reviewer inspected the assembled LaTeX, scientific evidence, figures and actual rendered pages. These review scopes are kept distinct.
