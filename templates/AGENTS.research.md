## ResearchFlow: research-to-paper coordination

For a substantive scientific research-to-paper project, use `$research-workflow-governor` and bounded multi-agent work when available. For isolated edits, use the relevant skill directly. Preserve the user's earlier questions, scope and existing authorization before acting on the final deliverable.

The main agent owns the evolving question, paper argument, priorities and shared research state. Specialists own disjoint outputs. Give each a question, purpose, claim scope, inputs, output paths, acceptance rationale, budget and return condition; do not send all history. Use PaperSpine early for journal/argument planning and later for writing; use simulation-project-orchestrator for simulation evidence.

Return after answering the contracted question. Two attempts without changing research understanding require coordinator reassessment, not automatic abandonment or automatic acceptance. Keep physical integrity and honest evidence; set accuracy by claims and use. Block only affected claims/dependencies and close consequential handoffs with requester interpretation. New counterevidence invalidates affected claims.

Persist plan, bounded tasks, evidence and dispositions with `python -m researchflow`; workers write separate results and the coordinator records them. Read needed originals and audit at consequential dispatch/promotion/final delivery. The runtime checks process and bindings; agents still judge the science.
